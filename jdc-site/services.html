How I Engage: Services and Offerings

Flexible engagement models designed for startups and lean organizations.

Jan 20, 2026 · Jake Davis

I offer three engagement models. All follow the PRISM methodology and include clear scoping, regular communication, and actionable deliverables.

Project-Based Consulting

Defined scope, fixed timeline, clear deliverables.

Best for: Specific analytical questions, one-time deep dives, strategic initiatives with defined endpoints.

Examples:

  • Customer segmentation analysis
  • Marketing attribution model
  • Churn prediction and intervention framework
  • Pricing optimization study
Typical Duration4–12 weeks
Typical Investment$15,000–$50,000

Retained Advisory

Ongoing analytical support with predictable monthly commitment.

Best for: Organizations that need regular analytical guidance but don't require (or can't justify) a full-time hire.

Includes:

  • Monthly strategic calls
  • Ad-hoc analysis requests
  • Methodology review and guidance
  • Priority access for urgent questions
Typical Commitment3–6 month minimum
Investment$5,000–$15,000/mo

Workshops & Training

Structured learning for teams building analytical capabilities.

Best for: Teams transitioning to data-driven decision making, analysts leveling up their skills, leadership seeking analytical literacy.

Topics include:

  • Statistical thinking for business decisions
  • Customer analytics fundamentals
  • A/B testing done right
  • Building an insights function
FormatHalf-day, full-day, or multi-session
InvestmentStarting at $5,000
