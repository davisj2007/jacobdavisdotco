Recent Work

Proof, not just a pitch

Case Study — Major CPG Brand, Marketing Effectiveness 4.8% Attributed Sales Lift

A major CPG brand needed to measure sales within a pharmacy retailer without holding out a significant sample of store locations. Causal inference modeling using external trends delivered a defensible lift estimate without the testing tradeoffs.

Case Study — Education Publishing Client, Lead Scoring Model Hand-raising beats email volume as the strongest signal of a qualified lead

Modeling prospect engagement data to score lead quality, intent signals — event attendance and explicit hand-raises — dominated the model, while email send and click volume carried almost no predictive weight. Sales follow-up was reprioritized around behavior, not inbox activity.
