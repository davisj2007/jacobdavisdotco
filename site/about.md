Good analytics answers a question that matters, in language the people deciding can use, with a recommendation they can act on.

I favor clarity over complexity and done over perfect. I use the simplest method that answers the question. If a cross-tab does the job, nobody needs machine learning. When a harder method is the right tool, I use it and explain it in the language of decision makers.

I'm skeptical of analytics theater: complex methods deployed to impress rather than inform. Data without context is noise. Dashboards without decisions are expensive screensavers.
