Three themes adapt well to case-based sessions for business and analytics programs: building data science practices, using data for action, and measurement you can defend.

Each case starts from a real decision a business had to make, the data it had, and what happened next. Students work the problem before they see the answer.

Guest lectures, workshops, and executive education are all open.
